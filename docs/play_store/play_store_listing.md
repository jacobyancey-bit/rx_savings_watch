### Full Description (≤ 4,000 chars)
```text
Stop overpaying for brand-name prescriptions or manually checking every few months to see if a generic is finally available.

Rx Savings Watch is a 100% free, privacy-first medication tracker and generic launch monitor powered by official U.S. government databases (FDA Orange Book, Drugs@FDA, OpenFDA NDC & Shortages, NLM RxNorm, DailyMed, and CMS NADAC).

KEY FEATURES

• Real-Time 2-Signal Generic Launch Alerts
Many apps trigger false alarms the day a patent is challenged or a generic is approved on paper—months or years before pharmacies actually stock it. Rx Savings Watch requires BOTH (1) official FDA therapeutic equivalence approval AND (2) verified commercial pharmacy distribution before alerting you.

• Clear Pharmacist Substitution Guidance
See the exact FDA-approved generic name to ask for, whether your pharmacist can usually substitute it automatically (AB-rated) or if a new doctor's prescription is needed, and known brand-name equivalents when viewing a generic.

• Official Dosage Forms & Strengths
Cleanly separated by exact FDA dosage form (Oral Tablet, Extended-Release Capsule, Oral Solution, Injectable, Topical, Inhaler, etc.) so you can pick the exact prescription on your bottle and calculate personalized annual copay savings.

• Fast, Typo-Tolerant FDA Search
Type any U.S. brand or generic medication name—even with minor spelling errors—and immediately verify it against official FDA and RxNorm records.

• Medication Reminders & OTC / Supplements
Set daily, specific-day, or monthly reminders with optional discreet lock-screen mode (hides medication names on notifications), track Taken Today, and organize vitamins or over-the-counter items alongside prescriptions.

• Caregiver Multi-Cabinet Support
Managing medications for aging parents, children, or multiple patients? Create separate medication cabinets on your device with isolated lists and labeled reminders—no account required.

• Active FDA Drug Shortage Monitoring
Get notified when your specific medication strength and dosage form is listed on the official FDA Drug Shortages database.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY — ZERO PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED
• No account, no login, no email required.
• Your medication list, reminders, and bottle scans stay strictly on your device and never touch a server.
• Anonymous update checks mix in decoy maintenance drug codes (k-anonymity) so network traffic cannot reveal your prescriptions.
• Move your cabinet to a new device anytime with a zero-server QR code transfer.

Informational only — not medical, clinical, or pharmacy advice. Always verify prescriptions, dosages, and generic substitutions with your prescribing doctor or licensed pharmacist.
```
